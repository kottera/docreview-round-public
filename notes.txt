Notes for the tide board volunteers. This is a made-up document for a
review round.

The office key is on the hook behind the chandlery door. Return it the same
morning, because the harbour master opens up at eight.

Order new chalk in spring. The tin should never hold fewer than ten sticks,
and the supplier needs two weeks.

The spare board is in the net loft. It is heavier than it looks, so ask
someone to help carry it down the ladder.

If the printed tables go missing, the harbour office keeps a copy, and the
standard port's figures are also on the noticeboard at the lifeboat station.
